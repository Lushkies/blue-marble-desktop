Blue Marble Desktop bundles the Mesa 3-D graphics library (opengl32.dll,
libEGL.dll) as a software OpenGL fallback for Windows on ARM64. Prebuilt
binaries are obtained from https://github.com/mmozeiko/build-mesa (see
build.ps1 for the pinned SHA-256 of opengl32.dll).
